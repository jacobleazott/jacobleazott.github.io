Langton's Ant

Overview

In college I fell in love with cellular automata , specifically Langton's Ant . Something about the deterministic behavior, the simplicity of the rules, and the organized chaos absolutely fascinated me. I wanted a program that would allow me to easily create my own rule sets, grids, and conditions. So using my very limited GUI skills, I built a very simple program that allows you to create your own Langton's Ant simulation. Being able to create your own movement rules, grids, colors, and even support for multiple ants. It featured the ability to render gif's of the simulation on linear or logarithmic scales.
