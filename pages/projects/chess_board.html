Chess Board

Overview

Chess is such a beautiful game, a battle of wits and strategy. In these 64 squares there is such a vast amount of beauty, potential, frustration, and joy. I set out to create a display piece that would capture the simplicity of the game and its potential. I fully 3D printed an entire chess board, fitted it with individually addressable LEDs and thousands of games. It then slowly replays through these games and gives a slightly abstract but fully decipherable viewing experience.
