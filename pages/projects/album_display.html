Album Display

Overview

This project grew from a love of music and an appreciation for the artwork that goes with it. However, with such a large collection of music it would become a game of favorites as to which albums I would display artwork for in physical form. So why not make it digital? Digital picture frames exist, but they are limited in size and shape. Plus wanting "metadata" for each album creates problems with prebuilt digital picture frames. So why not take a rasperry pi zero, an old monitor, and some lumber and make your own? The result is a 19 inch display that rotates through every song in my library.
