Christmas Tree

Overview

Full inspiration for this project goes to Matt Parker however, all code, design, and electronics were done from the ground up by me. What's the fun in a Christmas tree you didn't code and unit test yourself? The tree has taken many shapes and forms over the years and apartment moves, but come Christmas time I dust off my LEDs, and get this tree back to life. Adding more features, quality of life improvements, unit tests, and more Christmas spirit each year. There is something extrordinarily special about a quiet snowy evening, a hot buttered rum, and being SSH'd into your Christmas tree coding away.
